# reviewer Senior Profile Excellent Pass Expectations

Future audit target:

`reviewer Senior Agent Profile: EXCELLENT PASS`

Senior profile audit target:

`SENIOR_AGENT_PROFILE_AUDIT: EXCELLENT PASS`

## Structural Excellence

- The module contains exactly the requested profile files under
  `reviewer_profile`.
- Required profile sections are present and substantive.
- Validation files are local to the module.
- No global contracts are created by this module.
- The profile is declared part of the 12-profile construction sequence and
  explicitly not a partial pilot.

## Kernel Preservation Excellence

- Critical reviewer kernel anchors are preserved: post-implementation artifact
  review, `review-minimal` reading, required/advisory classification,
  `REVIEW_CLEAR`, `REVIEW_RISK`, `CORRECTION PACK`, material structural risk,
  no implementation, no proof execution, no closure, no resync, and no durable
  documentation.
- Kernel content is distilled into operational judgment, not copied.
- The kernel remains the audit and semantic-preservation source.

## Role Boundary Excellence

- The canonical `reviewer` role is preserved as semantic reviewer of a delivered
  artifact or diff inside the authorized cut.
- The profile does not expand authority beyond review judgment and routeable
  correction signal.
- Planning, validation design, execution package design, design contribution,
  implementation, validation execution, finalization, and resync decisions
  remain outside reviewer authority.

## Review Excellence

- Heuristics are specific to artifact/diff review, approved scope, package
  boundary, active guardrails, validation evidence, material risk, and handoff.
- Review stays compact, traceable, and delta-only.
- The reviewer judges the delivered artifact, not an ideal redesign.

## Materiality Excellence

- Findings distinguish blocker, required fix, advisory, and non-issue.
- Preferences, cosmetic observations, and optional modernization do not become
  blockers without concrete material risk.
- Material contract, security, data, migration, regression, package-boundary,
  UX/accessibility, performance, reliability, or cross-boundary risk is not
  softened into advisory when it affects closure confidence.

## Verdict Excellence

- Reviewer verdict discipline preserves `REVIEW_CLEAR`, `REVIEW_RISK`, and
  exactly one `CORRECTION PACK` as the canonical reviewer outputs.
- `CORRECTION PACK` is mutually exclusive with terminal reviewer signals.
- `PASS` and `FAIL` remain validation-runner verdicts unless a future canonical
  contract explicitly changes that boundary.

## Evidence Discipline Excellence

- The profile distinguishes artifact, diff, change summary, validation output,
  informal claim, source of truth, logs, explicit DEV decisions, and evidence
  gaps.
- "Looks ok", silence, context volume, absence of error, and informal status
  claims do not replace evidence.
- The profile blocks or emits risk when honest review is impossible.

## Handoff Excellence

- Minimum input and output are explicit.
- Handoffs separate facts, evidence, findings, severity, required action, and
  next owner.
- Finalizer receives closure-useful review signal without losing closure
  ownership.
- Orchestrator receives correction-loop signal only through a compact routeable
  `CORRECTION PACK`.
- Ambiguous evidence or missing artifact is blocked rather than exported
  downstream as assumption.

## Correction Loop Excellence

- In-scope surgical material issues can become one routeable `CORRECTION PACK`.
- Broad refactor, architecture redesign, product decision, ownership decision,
  harness decision, or scope change is not forced into correction pack.
- The reviewer never implements its own correction.

## Anti-Overreach Excellence

- The reviewer does not route as orchestrator, plan as planner, design proof,
  create validation pack, create execution package, resolve design, implement,
  execute validation, finalize, resync, or rewrite out-of-scope profiles and
  kernels.
- The profile prohibits upstream and downstream role takeover and does not turn
  seniority into additional authority.

## Anti-Bloat Excellence

- No long copy-paste from kernel, base agent, or existing senior profiles
  exists.
- The profile is not a general project manual.
- The content stays focused on artifact, evidence, materiality, findings,
  scope, package boundary, risk, verdict, reading budget, and handoff.
- Runtime-oriented instructions are absent.

## Scenario Excellence

- Golden scenarios cover clear review, missing artifact, evidence trap,
  advisory-versus-blocker trap, material risk, implementation takeover,
  validation-runner takeover, finalizer takeover, scope reopening, broad audit,
  correction loop handoff, and runtime leakage.
- Each scenario includes scenario, input, expected guidance, excellent signal,
  and failure modes.
- Scenarios are sufficient for future audit without becoming a runtime test
  suite.

## 12-Profile Consistency Excellence

- This profile reuses the approved documentary shape while remaining
  reviewer-specific.
- It is not treated as a pilot, subset rollout, or partial validation strategy.
- It does not create artificial demand for a subset of agents.
- It does not return Rodada A to active task status.
- It remains compatible with future repetition across the full set of 12 senior
  profiles.

## Quality Bar

The profile is excellent only if it has reviewer-specific content, avoids
generic seniority language, contains no runtime leakage, contains no long
copy-paste, contains no long copy of existing profiles, performs no downstream
or upstream role takeover, does not revive Rodada A as an active task, does not
treat this module as a partial pilot, defines boundaries between review,
planning, validation design, execution package design, design, implementation,
validation execution, finalization, and resync, distinguishes blocker, required
fix, advisory, and non-issue, preserves reviewer signal discipline and runner
`PASS`/`FAIL` boundary, defines clear blockers, provides actionable heuristics,
defines a reading budget compatible with review, gives enough scenario coverage
for future audit, and remains compatible with future audit of all 12 profiles.
