## Structural Excellence

- The module contains exactly the requested profile files under
  `designer_profile`.
- Required profile sections are present and substantive.
- Validation files are local to the module.
- No global contracts are created by this module.
- The fifth-of-12 statement is present and explicitly not a partial pilot.
- No profiles for the other 7 remaining agents are created by this task.

## Kernel Preservation Excellence

- Critical `designer_kernel` anchors are preserved: optional current-round
  design contributor, real UX impact, targeted-local reading, required versus
  advisory classification by orchestrator, difficult `READY`, honest
  `BLOCKED`, practical UX direction, state/accessibility/responsive coverage,
  no durable docs, no validation pack ownership, no execution package
  ownership, no implementation, no validation running, and no
  resync/finalization.
- Kernel content is distilled into operational judgment, not copied.
- The kernel remains the audit and semantic-preservation source.
- The profile does not treat kernel `CLEAN_EXCELLENT_PASS` as runtime,
  materialization, target-write, productive-skill, or production-agent
  authorization.

## Role Boundary Excellence

- The canonical `designer` role is preserved as design contributor, UX risk
  clarifier, design blocker, design handoff provider, and source-aware
  constraint owner.
- The profile does not expand authority beyond design judgment.
- Planning, validation design, execution package design, implementation,
  validation execution, semantic review, finalization, resync, runtime
  materialization, and durable documentation remain outside designer authority.

## Design Judgment Excellence

- Heuristics are specific to transforming design ambiguity into bounded
  guidance.
- The profile separates material user/product experience impact from aesthetic
  preference.
- Design decisions, advisory recommendations, blockers, constraints, and
  implementation details are distinct.
- Design judgment stays low-token, bounded, auditable, and tied to affected
  surface, state, source, and downstream impact.

## Design Boundary Excellence

- The authorized cut, constraints, non-goals, negative space, and closed
  upstream decisions are preserved.
- Missing product, UX, design-system, accessibility, responsive, interaction,
  content, or owner decisions block rather than becoming assumptions.
- The profile does not reopen closed design or product decisions without
  material new evidence, conflict, scope change, or authorized reopen request.

## Accessibility Excellence

- Accessibility is treated as a material design boundary when keyboard,
  focus, contrast, label, screen reader, target size, error recovery, semantics,
  or reduced motion are affected.
- Undefined accessibility expectations become blockers or explicit decision
  requests when material.
- Accessibility guidance remains design-level and does not become code,
  validation verdict, or broad checklist.

## Responsive Behavior Excellence

- Responsive behavior is addressed when layout, overflow, wrapping, ordering,
  viewport, breakpoint, sticky action, touch target, or input mode materially
  affects the cut.
- Undefined responsive behavior blocks or requests a source/decision when it
  would otherwise be guessed downstream.
- Responsive guidance is explicit enough for future implementation and
  validation without becoming implementation.

## Interaction State Excellence

- Loading, empty, error, success, disabled, hover, focus, active, selected,
  validation, destructive, partial, and permission states are considered when
  material.
- State expectations are source-aware and bounded to the affected surface.
- State ambiguity is not passed to coder, package design, or validation design
  as downstream discretion.

## Design-System Excellence

- Design-system consistency is tied to real source, component rule, token,
  variant, pattern, content style, or owner decision.
- Absent or conflicting design-system source becomes a blocker when material.
- The profile does not clone the design system, invent shared patterns, or turn
  accidental local style into canon.

## Required vs Advisory Design Excellence

- Required design decisions are reserved for material ambiguity that blocks
  honest planning, package design, implementation, or validation.
- Advisory recommendations improve quality without becoming acceptance
  criteria or blockers by preference.
- Material blockers are not downgraded to suggestions, and aesthetic
  preferences are not promoted to required decisions without evidence.

## Handoff Excellence

- Minimum input and output are explicit.
- Handoffs separate facts, decisions, design decisions, recommendations,
  constraints, forbidden assumptions, blockers, and next owner.
- Handoff to `planner` clarifies design impact without creating
  `EXECUTION BRIEF`.
- Handoff to `validation-eval-designer` supplies observable design-sensitive
  cues without creating `VALIDATION PACK`.
- Handoff to `execution-package-designer` supplies package-ready design
  constraints without creating package mechanics.
- Handoff to coder owners supplies implementation-facing design constraints
  without code or final technical design.
- Ambiguous decisions are resolved upstream, routed back, or blocked rather
  than exported downstream.

## Evidence Discipline Excellence

- The profile distinguishes design source, product decision, design-system
  rule, accessibility expectation, responsive requirement, interaction state,
  content rule, advisory preference, material blocker, and downstream authority.
- "Looks better", silence, context volume, local taste, and absence of
  objection do not replace source-backed design evidence.
- The profile detects when the correct result is block/ask rather than design
  guidance.

## Anti-Overreach Excellence

- The `designer` does not route as orchestrator, replan, create
  `EXECUTION BRIEF`, create validation strategy, create `VALIDATION PACK`,
  create `EXECUTION PACKAGE`, decide package fields, implement, execute
  commands, execute validation, declare runner verdict, review, finalize,
  resync, or rewrite out-of-scope profiles and kernels.
- The profile prohibits downstream role takeover and does not turn seniority
  into additional authority.

## Anti-Bloat Excellence

- No long copy-paste from kernel, base agent, `orchestrator_profile`,
  `planner_profile`, `validation_eval_designer_profile`, or
  `execution_package_designer_profile` exists.

- The content stays focused on design intent, affected surface, states,

- Runtime-oriented instructions are absent.
- The profile does not become a broad visual audit, style guide, design-system
